About

I am a PhD student in mathematics at Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin, affiliated with the Berlin Mathematical School and supervised by Thomas Walpuski. I expect to graduate in the summer of 2027.

My research lies in differential geometry and geometric analysis, with particular emphasis on manifolds with special holonomy, calibrated submanifolds, Fueter maps, and multi-valued harmonic spinors and forms.
